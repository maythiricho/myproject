Universal Studio

Where magic happens and memories are made!

About This Wonderful Place

Welcome to Universal Studios Singapore, the ultimate theme park destination that brings the magic of movies to life! Get ready for thrilling rides, captivating shows, and immersive attractions that will create unforgettable memories. This world-class park offers something for everyone, from adrenaline-pumping adventures to family-friendly fun.

Location

Universal Studios Singapore is located on Sentosa Island, Singapore. It is a major attraction within Resorts World Sentosa.

"I had a blast at Universal Studios Singapore!"

- An Excited Visitor

Best Time to Visit

The best time to visit Universal Studios Singapore is generally during the weekdays to avoid large crowds. The park is open year-round, but avoid public holidays for a more relaxed experience.

Top Things to Do in Universal Studios Singapore

  • Experience Thrilling Rides (Embark on exhilarating roller coasters and adventure rides based on your favorite movies!)
  • Watch Spectacular Shows (Enjoy live performances, water shows, and character meet-and-greets!)
  • Explore Themed Zones (Immerse yourself in different movie worlds, from Sci-Fi City to Ancient Egypt!)
  • Dine and Shop (Savor delicious food and find unique souvenirs!)

Fun Things You Absolutely Must Do!

  • Take a Scenic Hot Air Balloon Ride (See the world from a new perspective!)
  • Explore the Crystal Caves (Glow-in-the-dark wonders await!)
  • Indulge in a Local Cooking Class (Become a culinary wizard!)
  • Hike to the Whispering Waterfall (Legend says it grants wishes!)
